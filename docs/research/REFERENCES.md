## Dishoom

Source: https://www.dishoom.com/ . Observed a dark media-led opening, serif text, compact wordmark, clear booking access; navigation separates cafés, menus and stories. Page content connects food with Bombay culture and captions, then provides menu and visit paths. Design lesson: culture is communicated through specific evidence and editorial sequencing. Adaptation: actual tandoori process and source-linked dishes, short contextual captions and visible Menu/Visit actions. Avoid importing Bombay history, a multi-location mega-menu, shop or charity/award material. A slow immersive opener should not delay Tandoori Junction's menu task.

## Gymkhana

Requested: https://gymkhanalondon.com/ ; observed final https://gymkhanarestaurants.com/ . Full-frame hospitality imagery, restrained ivory serif type, dark wood/red upholstery, compact navigation and a small reservation action. Story, food imagery and location/contact content form distinct chapters. Cultural positioning is explicitly tied to its own club inspiration. Lesson: atmosphere and service photography carry premium character without dense decoration. Adaptation: disciplined image cropping, concise chapter text, calm hierarchy. Do not copy club identity, crest, clipped plaques, stars, imagery or reservation service. Its overlays competed for space in the observed desktop hero; avoid that distraction here. Mobile remained unverified.

## Indian Accent, New Delhi

Source: https://www.indianaccent.com/newdelhi . Bright overhead food imagery, quiet background, sparse location navigation. Menu variants and contact information are explicit; cuisine, chef and private dining have separate content sections. Lesson: strong food photography and direct menu labelling earn credibility. Adaptation: visible dish identities, menu taxonomy and practical contact close, without importing biographies, awards or dietary menus. Narrow capture showed a tall food crop, with header location links absent in the captured region and visible horizontal overflow at the bottom; treat it as a caution, not a mobile pattern to reproduce. Full mobile interaction not audited.

## Translation decisions (all proposed)

| Principle | Value for Tandoori Junction | Original adaptation | Risk / control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Specific cultural storytelling | Credibility | Cooking and food evidence from the menu; owner story only when obtained | No invented heritage |
| Varied image scale | Appetite and pace | Wide tandoor image, single dish study, street-food diptych | Actual food assets required |
| Editorial typography | Memorable hierarchy | Cormorant headlines with Manrope functional layer | Verify thin strokes at mobile sizes |
| Distinct menu access | Fast decision | Primary Menu CTA; source-preserving searchable HTML menu | No PDF-only menu |
| Practical visit close | Useful conversion | Confirmed address/phone/hours and directions | Hours unknown; no open-now claim |
| Restrained service paths | Commercial clarity | Dine-in/takeout/catering with confirmed destinations | No fictional booking/provider |
