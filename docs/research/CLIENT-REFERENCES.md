## King Tandoori deep review — 2026-10-18

Source: https://kingtandoori.ca/ and https://kingtandoori.ca/menu/. The user directly requested another detailed study and a simpler light Tandoori Junction design. I inspected the live desktop homepage at about 1280px, the live menu's page content, and a 390px mobile screenshot.

| Observed on King Tandoori | Why it works | Tandoori Junction adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White header with red call-to-action, dark labels and generous blank space | Establishes a familiar restaurant feel and gives practical navigation an obvious place | White header/canvas, original red/yellow TJ logo, dark system-sans links and one red Menu action |
| Large close-up cooking/food image leads the homepage, with short high-contrast headline | Makes food the proof and the first focus | Use the supplied TJ spread as a static hero image; keep copy short and place it beside the image |
| Menu is available from the first navigation row and has an order shortcut | Reduces the distance between appetite and menu | Keep Menu primary; omit order shortcut because no Tandoori Junction ordering provider is confirmed |
| Menu landing starts with direct category links, then gives dish names, prices and photos | Lets visitors browse visually and still compare actual items | Remove search; show a matching TJ PDF category photo, original category labels, dish names, descriptions and printed prices in each section |
| Desktop and mobile switch to compact navigation; mobile menu begins with a food-image banner and category shortcuts | Preserves food context while menu navigation remains understandable on a phone | Keep native compact navigation and simple vertical category anchors; ensure no horizontal overflow |

Tradeoffs visible in the reference: the desktop header carries many links and ordering/franchise/promotions that do not apply here; the mobile menu capture displayed a horizontal scrollbar; the very long catalogue relies on repeated item images and can become visually repetitive. Tandoori Junction will keep a compact four-page navigation, no invented order service, no competitor material, and a small number of authentic category photographs. This is a design and content observation, not a full accessibility, performance or cross-device audit.

The Tandoori Junction proof contains one wide food banner per menu page. Each banner depicts a category spread and the current source provides no verified photo asset for every one of the 169 entries. Use it as the opening photo for the corresponding category section, never as a claim that it depicts each dish in the list.
