## Data rules (proposal, no import implemented)

- Future item: stable ID, source name/description, approved display name/description separately, page, printed order, status, review notes, categories, availability, image reference.
- Future price: decimal amount / currency, variant label, source page, scope (item/category), confirmation status, confirmed date. No floating-point money. Keep printed string too.
- Future options: distinguish size, filling, preparation and protein. Slash-separated source text remains unresolved until approved; do not create combinations/prices automatically.
- All rows require owner confirmation before production. Q01–Q09 specifically block affected ambiguous fields. Printed Veg/Non Veg headings are source taxonomy only; no inferred vegan, gluten-free, halal, allergen-free or spice badges.
- Four likely cross-listings: P04-13↔P11-02, P04-14↔P11-03, P04-15↔P11-06, P04-16↔P11-05. Matching price is evidence, not proof of identical portions. Preserve both records until owner confirms a shared item or separate meal.
- Search matches approved names/descriptions and editorial spelling aliases; aliases do not silently rename original records. Preserve meaningful differences such as curry Chilli Chicken versus Hakka Chicken 65.
- No description is preferable to invented copy. Additions require content approval.
