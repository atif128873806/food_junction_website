# Inner pages — current light direction

All inner pages share the white canvas, near-black system-sans text, restrained Tandoori Junction red, the original logo, compact navigation and footer. Each page answers one practical question. Use a supplied category photo only where it helps.

## Menu — current 2026-10-21 reading concept

Present the 12 source groups as a visible table of contents. Desktop uses a sticky left index; narrow screens use a sticky horizontal rail with 48px category targets and the current section kept in view. Every selection remains a native, shareable `?category=` link that renders one complete group. The active state is exposed through `aria-current`. Within a group, offer jump links when multiple original leaf headings exist, and previous/next group links below the dishes. Keep the matching PDF banner uncropped and labelled as a category image, then use a single reading column for source dish names, descriptions, aligned printed prices/variants and review notes. Keep the print-price warning near the introduction. No search or ordering UI.

## Our Story

Keep it short and factual: introduce the Markham restaurant through its printed menu range and hospitality services, with one actual menu photo. Do not fabricate owners, founding year, recipes, staff, cultural lineage or awards. Link to the full menu and Visit Us.

## Catering

Explain that catering is offered, show the real food spread and make the phone number the clear inquiry path. Avoid forms, packages, lead times, service areas, pricing, event-capacity claims and reservation behavior until confirmed.

## Visit Us

Show the menu-printed address and phone clearly. Include a directions link based on the printed address and tell visitors to call for current hours. Do not claim a verified map pin, open-now status, parking or accessibility facilities.

## Shared behavior

Native GET links, the category disclosure, readable list content and phone links work without JavaScript. Maintain skip navigation, visible focus, descriptive alt text, 44px interactive targets, responsive stacking and reduced-motion support. No horizontal scrolling at 320–390px.
