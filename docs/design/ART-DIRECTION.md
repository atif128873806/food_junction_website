# Art direction — two original alternatives

Status: proposed 2026-10-17. Neither direction, typography nor working message is approved. Both preserve the supplied brand name and existing logo, meet the dark palette brief and use source-grounded content. No application code or generated food imagery. These are original proposals derived from evidence; not copies of reference layouts.

## A — Ember & Heritage (APPROVED)

Thesis: make tandoori craft the emotional entry point, then unfold the breadth of a welcoming Markham restaurant. Heritage means real dishes and cooking practices evidenced in the menu; it does not imply an undocumented founding story.

World: charcoal as the dominant canvas; warm ivory type; ember-red action fields; copper used sparingly for fine rules and captions; walnut in service/visit sections. Materials are real char, glazed food, clay and dark tabletops captured photographically, rather than synthetic textures, floating spices or ornamental wallpaper. Existing red/yellow logo remains unchanged.

Typography: Cormorant Garamond 500/600 display, Manrope 400/500/600 interface and menu. Large but measured headline; generous line spacing and short factual body copy. Body text remains upright and clear. No script imitation from the menu cover. Font choice is provisional until actual-device specimens pass.

First viewport at 1440: 88px solid-charcoal masthead; approved logo left, ordinary navigation, View Menu action. Below, 1280px content field aligned 80px from each edge. Left five columns: small “Tandoori Junction · Markham”, proposed two-line editorial message, factual service line, View Menu and Plan Your Visit. Right seven columns: close, directional-lit actual-client tandoori cooking/finished food image, tall crop continuing near viewport bottom. The photograph has a deliberate dark left edge so type need not sit over busy food. Hero image takes roughly 55–60% of the composition; story copy ends before the food focal point. No central floating plate or carousel. A small source-grounded dish caption adds specificity. Exact photo and crop drive final refinement.

First viewport at 390: 76–88px header with original logo, visible Menu link and labelled navigation toggle. Text comes before a 4:5 or 5:4 food crop with no overlay required. H1 approximately 44–52px and 2–3 lines; 20px gutters. Primary Menu action visible without a long animated introduction. Natural scroll; no viewport-height trap or sticky booking bar. Prototype must test the complete first screen at 390×844, with taller hero allowed when content needs it.

Visitor path: Menu → verified dishes; Visit → factual logistics; Catering → inquiry. From broad brand opening, the page becomes progressively more practical. Tandoori visual prominence never hides Hakka, quick meals or vegetarian choices.

Signature interaction: one restrained photograph reveal and short text line entrance; content is visible without JS. Later signature dish rows focus the corresponding photograph on hover AND keyboard focus without changing reading order. No essential content available only through hover; mobile stacks a small image with each selected dish instead of imitating hover.

Across pages: same ink/ivory hierarchy; menu is a readable price ledger; Our Story uses only obtained facts; Catering uses a shared-table spread; Visit gets strong logistical contrast. No “luxury hotel” styling or awards implied.

Honest risk: dark food imagery can become muddy and Cormorant thin strokes can disappear. Mitigation: actual photo shoot with controlled highlights, no heavy global dark overlay, weight 500/600 minimum, bounded headline size and real mobile rendering tests. Without credible photography, this direction cannot reach its intended quality.

## B — The Junction Table (SUPERSEDED)

Thesis: centre gathering and the generous range of the menu, using an editorial table setting as the organizing idea. “Junction” means the restaurant's meeting place; it does not invent railway history or a cultural origin story.

World: walnut and charcoal panels dominate; warm ivory reading surfaces occupy selected menu/story chapters, with deep ember typographic fields. Copper is limited to separators. More convivial and graphic than A, while remaining dark overall. No gold luxury treatment. Identity uses the original logo and the honest abundance of thalis, breads, curries, drinks and street food.

Typography: Fraunces 600 display, Manrope body. Compact, broad headlines; strong alignments; numbered content chapters and captioned photographs. Use numbers only for actual page sequence, never invented heritage dates. No ticket graphics, faux stamps or distressed paper.

First viewport at 1440: dark masthead over an asymmetric triptych. Left narrow column carries location/services and the primary menu action. Centre holds a large overhead photograph of an actual shared meal. Right carries one short, large proposed headline “Come to the table.” on walnut, followed by Visit. Text sits beside the image, not across plates. This is a three-part editorial composition rather than a hero background with floating text.

First viewport at 390: logo/navigation; concise welcome and Menu action; dominant overhead crop; service line. Three-part desktop hierarchy becomes a single linear story without sideways movement. Fraunces headline approximately 42–48px, image crop selected independently for mobile.

Visitor path: explore cuisine families immediately beneath the table image; quick meals/street food appear earlier in the visual hierarchy than A. Source-required eight chapters retain their sequence, but the intro and signature chapters use table-setting compositions. Service choice reads as a hospitality index rather than three promotional cards.

Signature interaction: focus/hover on a cuisine-family label reveals a static crop in a shared media region; explicit text link remains the destination. Mobile shows labelled image/text pairs. No autoplay carousel.

Across pages: bold type and tabular menu layout; catering naturally extends the communal-table image; visit information uses the clearest ivory reading panel. Story can be a brief factual introduction without inventing a founder narrative.

Honest risk: more graphic and convivial, less directly cinematic; overhead spreads can appear generic without actual-client art direction. Requires carefully styled real food and disciplined composition to avoid becoming a broad restaurant catalogue.

## Recommendation and reasons

Recommend A, Ember & Heritage. The logo already centres flame/cutlery; pages 5–6 devote 30 entries to tandoori preparations and repeatedly describe tandoor cooking. Charcoal and red extend the print identity rather than replacing recognition. A gives the name and food craft an immediate visual reason to be remembered, while quieter menu/visit layouts preserve commercial usability. B communicates the broad everyday offering strongly but gives less prominence to the most distinctive name/food connection.

Reference principles adapted: Dishoom's evidence-led cultural storytelling, Gymkhana's disciplined image/type hierarchy, Indian Accent's food and menu clarity. Their exact styling, narratives, awards and reservation flows are excluded. Existing brand recognition and customer tasks outrank any generic cinematic technique in a skill.
